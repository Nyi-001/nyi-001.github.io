01.

about

I'm a third-year Cyber Security student at the University of Computer Studies, Yangon, with a deep interest in offensive security. My goal is to become a red teamer — understanding systems well enough to find what others miss.

Most of my time goes into web security: PortSwigger labs, hands-on practice, and my own tooling like SP1D3R. I also play CTFs — 4th place at the Myanmar Cyber Security Challenge 2026, 8th at MCSC 2025 and 7th at NovaCTF 2025 — and publish writeups of the challenges I solve.

On the defensive side, I'm building knowledge in digital forensics — network investigation and endpoint analysis — because a good attacker understands the investigator.
